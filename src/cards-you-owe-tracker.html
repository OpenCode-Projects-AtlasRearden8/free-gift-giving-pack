A calmer gifting printable

Cards You Owe

A birthday sneaks by, a thank-you waits three weeks, and the guilt gets louder than the kindness. This little tracker keeps every card — birthday, sympathy, thank-you, just-because — moving from your head to the mailbox.

How to use it

  1. Any time a card occasion comes up — or slips past — add a row. No judgment; this sheet has seen everything.
  2. Tick the boxes in order: card picked, words written, note sent.
  3. Write the date sent, so you can stop carrying it in your head.
  4. Use the Thank-You Note Templates sheet when the words won't start.
  5. Keep page 3 handy: addresses at a glance, plus a drawer-stash tip.

What's inside

  • Page 2 — the tracker: who, what occasion, and three friendly checkboxes.
  • Page 3 — addresses at a glance and a few kind reminders.

A kind note: a late card still counts. The person on the other end doesn't see the date you meant to send it — they see that you thought of them at all. Mail it whenever it's ready.

The drawer stash (a small act of future kindness)

  • Keep 3 blank cards, a book of stamps, and a working pen in one drawer.
  • One birthday card bought early is one fewer “oh no” later.
  • Batch it: pick five cards one evening, and the week writes itself.

Worth remembering: a “no reason, just thinking of you” card is the rarest kind — and usually the one people keep in a drawer of their own for years.
