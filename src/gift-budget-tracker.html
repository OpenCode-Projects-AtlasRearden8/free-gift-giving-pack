Gift Budget Tracker

Generosity feels best when it has a gentle plan behind it. This tracker helps you decide — on purpose, in advance — how much feels right for each person, so the season stays warm instead of expensive.

How to use it

  1. List every person and occasion you buy for — birthdays, holidays, thank-yous, the neighbor who always shovels your walk.
  2. Write the amount you'd plan to spend before you shop. Round, honest numbers are fine: $10, $25, “about $40.”
  3. As you buy, fill in Spent. The Left column does the worrying for you.
  4. Use page 3 to keep a season total, so one big day never surprises you.
  5. If a plan changes mid-season — and it will — just update it kindly and move on.

What's inside

  • Page 2 — the per-person tracker: plan, spend, and what's left.
  • Page 3 — season totals, gentle tips, and a note for tight months.

A kind note: a budget isn't a limit on love. It's a promise to your future self that the joy of giving won't turn into the stress of paying for it. People remember thoughtfulness far longer than price tags.

Print at 100% (“Actual size”), US Letter, portrait. Prints nicely in black & white. Personal use: print as many copies as you like.

Gentle tips that actually help

  • Decide the season total first, then split it per person — it's easier to shrink a list than to un-spend money.
  • Keep a small “surprise fund” line for the gifts you didn't see coming (hostess gifts, the coworker exchange).
  • Buy a few steady, crowd-pleasing spares early. Future-you will call this genius.

If money is tight this year: handmade treats, a framed photo, a coupon book of favors, or a long letter cost little and land deep. The people who love you would rather you stayed cozy than went broke.
