A calmer gifting printable

Gift Idea Log

People tell us what they love all year long — in passing, at the store, on the phone. This sheet catches those little mentions the moment they happen, so December-you can relax while everyone else is still guessing.

How to use it

  1. Keep one sheet on the fridge, in your planner, or taped inside a cupboard door — somewhere you will actually see it.
  2. When someone says “I love these” or lingers over something, write it down that day. A date and a place (“at Grandma's”) help future-you remember why it mattered.
  3. Before a birthday or holiday, skim the log instead of starting from zero. The best ideas are usually already written down.
  4. Tick the little box only when the gift is wrapped and in your hands — then enjoy that satisfying check mark.
  5. Print a fresh sheet each season, or keep one running log for the whole year. Both work. There is no wrong way to use this.

What's inside

  • Page 2 — the year-round log: who mentioned what, when, and whether it's done.
  • Page 3 — “Little details worth keeping”: sizes, favorites, hobbies, and things to skip.

A kind note: noticing what people love is already a gift. You don't need the perfect present — you just need to have been paying attention, and this sheet proves you were.

Print at 100% (“Actual size”), US Letter, portrait. Prints nicely in black & white. Personal use: print as many copies as you like.
