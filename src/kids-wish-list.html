A calmer gifting printable

Kids Wish List

A wish list is a little piece of a kid's imagination on paper. This one gives them room to dream out loud — and gives grown-ups a clear, kind answer for relatives who ask, “So what do they want?”

How to use it

  1. Print one sheet per child. Younger kids can dictate wishes and draw pictures; big kids can fill it in themselves.
  2. Rank the wishes — number 1 first. The order tells you what really matters (and what's just commercial-break enthusiasm).
  3. The “Why I'd love it” column is the magic one. It turns a list of stuff into a list of stories.
  4. Share the sheet (or a photo of it) with grandparents, aunts, and uncles, or bring it along for holiday planning.
  5. Grown-ups: pair this with the Gift Budget Tracker to match wishes with real numbers, kindly.

What's inside

  • Page 2 — the wish list: name, biggest wish, and ranked wishes.
  • Page 3 — more wishes, plus a grown-up corner for notes and surprises.

A kind note for kids: every wish on this page is allowed to be big, small, silly, or shy. Wishes can change — that's not being fickle, that's being a person. And no matter what arrives, being thought of is already something wonderful.

Print at 100% (“Actual size”), US Letter, portrait. Prints nicely in black & white. Personal use: print as many copies as you like.
